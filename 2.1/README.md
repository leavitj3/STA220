# STA 220, Module 2.1: the GSS files

The CSV files in this folder hold data from the 2024 General Social Survey (GSS).

## Where did the data come from?

The GSS is a project of NORC at the University of Chicago, funded mainly by the National Science
Foundation. The public-use data are free to download at https://gss.norc.org. NORC asks anyone who
uses the data to cite it this way:

Davern, Michael; Bautista, Rene; Freese, Jeremy; Herd, Pamela; and Morgan, Stephen L. General Social
Survey 1972-2024. [Machine-readable data file]. Principal Investigator, Michael Davern; Co-Principal
Investigators, Rene Bautista, Jeremy Freese, Pamela Herd, and Stephen L. Morgan. NORC ed. Chicago,
2026. 1 datafile and 1 codebook (2024 Release 3a).

That is the citation as NORC posted it in November 2026, with the rest of its terms for using GSS
data: https://gss.norc.org/terms-and-conditions
